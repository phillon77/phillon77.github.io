Private by design

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 25, 2026

不建立開發者帳號，也不追蹤

Plant Buddy 不需要另外建立 Plant Buddy 帳號。我們不使用分析、廣告或追蹤，也不將植物資料傳送到 Plant Buddy 經營的伺服器。

本機與你的 iCloud

植物的文字資料與照片會保存在裝置上，並使用 Apple CloudKit 同步到你 Apple 帳號的 iCloud 私人資料庫。這些資料用於你自己的裝置間同步，不會成為公開資料。

沒有網路或暫時無法連上 iCloud 時，App 會使用本機資料，讓你繼續離線操作；連線恢復後會再同步。你可在 iOS「設定」中管理 Apple 帳號、iCloud Drive 與 Plant Buddy 的 iCloud 權限。

相機、照片、通知與小工具

只有在你選擇拍照時才會要求相機權限；選擇現有照片使用 Apple 系統照片選擇器。照護提醒使用本機通知。主畫面小工具是唯讀，只讀取 App 準備的摘要並開啟 App，不會建立照護資料。

聯絡方式

若資料處理方式改變，我們會更新本政策與生效日期。請聯絡 lzz.1999@gmail.com。

No developer account and no tracking

Plant Buddy requires no separate Plant Buddy account. We use no analytics, advertising, or tracking, and do not send plant data to servers operated by Plant Buddy.

Local storage and your iCloud

Plant text data and photos are stored on the device and synchronized with Apple CloudKit to the iCloud Private Database for your Apple Account. This supports synchronization across your own devices and does not make the data public.

When the network or iCloud is temporarily unavailable, the app uses the local copy so you can continue offline; synchronization resumes after access returns. Manage your Apple Account, iCloud Drive, and Plant Buddy’s iCloud access in iOS Settings.

Camera, photos, notifications, and Widget

Camera access is requested only when you choose to take a photo; existing-photo selection uses Apple’s system photo picker. Care reminders use local notifications. The Home Screen Widget is read-only: it reads an app-prepared summary and opens the app, but does not create care data.

Contact

We will update this policy and its effective date if data practices change. Contact lzz.1999@gmail.com.
