Getting started

Need a hand?

Plant Buddy keeps a local copy and synchronizes through your iCloud Private Database.

建立植物與照護紀錄

  • 每盆植物都能保存名稱、種類、位置、備註、主要照片，以及澆水、施肥和檢查紀錄。
  • 成長照片可直接使用相機拍攝，或從 Apple 系統照片選擇器加入。

iCloud 同步與離線使用

植物的文字資料與照片會儲存在裝置上，並同步到你 Apple 帳號的 iCloud 私人資料庫。沒有網路或暫時無法連上 iCloud 時，仍可離線使用本機資料；連線恢復後會自動繼續同步。

你可以在 iOS「設定」中管理 Apple 帳號、iCloud Drive 與 Plant Buddy 的 iCloud 權限。關閉 iCloud 不會刪除當下的本機資料，但會暫停跨裝置同步。

主畫面小工具

小工具是唯讀，只顯示接下來需要檢查的植物。點選項目會開啟 App 中對應的植物檢查畫面；小工具不會建立或修改照護紀錄。

提醒與支援

植物檢查與施肥提醒使用本機通知。若仍有問題，請寄信給 lzz.1999@gmail.com。

Add plants and care records

  • Each plant can store its name, species, location, notes, primary photo, watering, fertilizer, and check history.
  • Add growth photos with the camera or Apple’s system photo picker.

iCloud synchronization and offline use

Plant text data and photos are kept on the device and synchronized to the iCloud Private Database for your Apple Account. When the network or iCloud is temporarily unavailable, you can keep working offline with the local copy; synchronization resumes automatically after access returns.

Manage your Apple Account, iCloud Drive, and Plant Buddy’s iCloud access in iOS Settings. Turning off iCloud does not delete the current local copy, but cross-device synchronization pauses.

Home Screen Widget

The Widget is read-only and shows plants that need a check next. Tapping an item opens the app at that plant’s check screen; the Widget never creates or changes a care record.

Reminders and support

Plant checks and fertilizer reminders use local notifications. For more help, email lzz.1999@gmail.com.
